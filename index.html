Galaxy Community Hub redesign

Three design directions

Each prototype mocks the same ten pages with the same real content, so you can compare them side by side.

A · Brand evolution

Today's Hub look, cleaned up: dark sidebar, calmer and denser type, a sticky "On this page" list, and a sidebar that shows which region you are in.

B · Bold

A lighter, editorial take: white top bar with a region picker, light left menu, serif headings, denser news and event rows.

M · Brand-safe top bar

A middle path: today's Galaxy colours and font with B's top-bar navigation, region picker and page layouts.

Static mock-ups for discussion, not the live site. Feedback on what works, what doesn't, and which parts you would combine is very welcome.
