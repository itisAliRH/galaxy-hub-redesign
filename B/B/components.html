Tokens and every component of the Bold direction, in light and dark, with their states. All colour pairs are computed from the role tokens in styles.css.

Colour

Brand-derived scales: Galaxy blue re-tuned a touch cooler and more saturated than #25537b so links read fresher; ink replaces the dark sidebar navy as the text colour; gold stays #ffd700 for fills and gets a dark ochre (gold-800) for text. Components only use role tokens, each declared once as light-dark(light, dark).

Light and dark

The theme follows prefers-color-scheme by default. The System / Light / Dark switch (top bar at ≥1024 px, menu sheet below) sets data-theme on <html> and remembers it in localStorage["hub-proto-theme"]; ?theme=dark|light in the URL wins for that view and is carried on internal links. data-theme on any element re-themes its subtree, which is how the side-by-side panes below work.

In dark the white chrome (top bar, left nav, sheet) becomes a dark surface one step above the page, the gold brand line stays, the Hub logo swaps to its white variant, and logos with dark lettering sit on a light plate. The serif is re-tuned: titles drop from 600 to 560 and open up slightly, because light-on-dark strokes look heavier, and use a warm paper white (--display-ink). Links move to a lighter, more saturated blue (#86b5f0), and the primary button flips to a light-blue fill with a dark label.
