Galaxy Community Hub redesign · prototype A

Brand evolution

Today's Galaxy Hub look, cleaned up: the dark sidebar, Atkinson Hyperlegible and a scarce gold accent stay. Type is calmer and denser, articles get a flat reading surface with a sticky "On this page" list, and the sidebar shows which region you are in, with region and global links in separate groups.

Things to try

  • Open Galaxy Freiburg, then Community → The Galaxy Community. Watch how the page tells you that you left Freiburg, and use "Back to Galaxy Freiburg".
  • Open the region picker and move through it with the keyboard.
  • Switch between light and dark with the theme control.
  • Make the window narrow (or open it on a phone) and try the menu.
  • Scroll the Galaxy Community article at full width: the "On this page" list stays in view.
